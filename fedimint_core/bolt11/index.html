Mapping between Bitcoin networks and BOLT11 invoice currencies Mapping between Bitcoin networks and BOLT11 invoice currencies.

BOLT11 uses the tb prefix for both testnet3 and testnet4, so Currency::BitcoinTestnet cannot be converted back to a single Network. The From conversions in lightning-invoice are lossy in both directions (Network::Testnet4 becomes Currency::Regtest), so use these helpers instead.
